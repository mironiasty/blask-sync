Why Blask Sync?

Philips' own Hue Sync app for Mac is no longer supported. Blask Sync is a free, open-source Hue Sync alternative that keeps your Philips Hue lights in sync with your screen and music on today's macOS.

Three ways to light up

Pick a mode from the menu bar. That's it.

Choose Screen, Music or Manual and press Start. Blask Sync streams colors to your lights over the Hue Entertainment API, so they react without lag.

Screen

Strips behind your monitor follow its edges and lamps around it pick up the mood of the scene.

  • Captures up to 60 frames per second
  • Smooth easing with four intensity levels
  • Saturation boost keeps colors vivid

Music

Your lights ripple with whatever your Mac is playing: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, anything.

  • Beat, bass, mid and treble detection
  • Flow and Pulse styles, four palettes
  • No virtual audio driver to install

Manual

Paint your lights yourself on a color wheel with a pin for every light, or pick a ready-made scene.

  • Recolor lights one by one or together
  • Warm, neutral and cool white scenes
  • Colors stay on after you quit
Made for your setup

Every light where it really is.

Drag-and-drop light mapping

Place each light around a picture of your monitor. Each one follows the part of the screen you point it at.

Find lights while you move them

While you arrange them, each real light glows in the color of its marker, so you always know which is which.

Gradient strips, segment by segment

Every segment of a Hue gradient lightstrip, like the Hue Play gradient lightstrip behind your monitor, gets its own color. Lamps beside the screen blend toward the whole picture.

Back to normal when you stop

Stop syncing and your lights return to how they were before, or switch off if you prefer.

Lives in the menu bar

No windows in your way. Sync keeps running in the background, and the icon lights up while it does.

Private by design

Everything happens on your Mac and your home network. Your screen and audio never leave your computer.

Get started

Up and glowing in two minutes.

Download

Get the latest zip, unzip it and move Blask Sync to your Applications folder.

Allow it to open

The app isn't notarized by Apple yet. Run this once in Terminal, or click Open Anyway in Privacy & Security.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/BlaskSync.app

Pair your bridge

Blask Sync finds your Hue Bridge on the network. Press the button on the bridge when asked.

Start syncing

Pick Screen or Music in the menu bar and press Start Sync. Allow screen or audio access the first time.

FAQ

Questions and answers.

Is Hue Sync still supported on Mac?

No. Philips' own Hue Sync app for Mac is no longer supported. Blask Sync is a free, open-source alternative that keeps your Philips Hue lights in sync with your Mac's screen and music on current versions of macOS.

What does Blask Sync do?

It's a menu bar app with three modes. Screen sync turns lights behind and around your monitor into an ambilight that follows the picture. Music sync makes your lights move with whatever your Mac plays. Manual lets you set each light's color on a color wheel.

Is Blask Sync free?

Yes. Blask Sync is free and open source under the MIT license. There's no account, subscription or in-app purchase.

What do I need to use it?

A Mac with macOS 14.2 or later (Apple Silicon or Intel), a Philips Hue Bridge v2 or Hue Bridge Pro, and an entertainment area set up in the Hue app.

Which Hue lights work with Blask Sync?

Any color lights you add to a Hue entertainment area, such as lightstrips, Hue Play light bars and lamps. Gradient lightstrips like the Hue Play gradient lightstrip get a separate color for every segment.

Can my Hue lights react to music on a Mac?

Yes. Music sync listens to everything your Mac plays, including Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube, without installing an extra audio driver. The lights follow the beat, bass, mids and treble, in your choice of palette and style.

Does Blask Sync turn my lights off when my Mac sleeps?

Yes. When your Mac sleeps or its display turns off, sync stops and the lights switch off. When you wake your Mac, sync or the lights come back, fading in gently. You can turn this off in Settings.

Is my screen or audio sent anywhere?

No. Your screen and audio are processed on your Mac and never leave it. Blask Sync sends only colors to your Hue Bridge on your home network.

Why does macOS ask before opening Blask Sync the first time?

Blask Sync isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks the first launch of a downloaded copy. Run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/BlaskSync.app in Terminal once, or click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

How do I update Blask Sync?

From version 0.2.0 on, Blask Sync checks for updates once a day and can install them for you. You can also choose Check for Updates… in the menu bar.
