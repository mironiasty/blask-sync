Blask Sync

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Blask Sync collects no data. It has no account, no analytics and no ads. Your screen and the audio your Mac plays are analyzed on your Mac and never stored or sent anywhere. Only light colors go to your Hue Bridge, on your own network.

What stays on your Mac

  • Screen. For screen sync, Blask Sync captures a small, low-resolution picture of the display you choose, works out the average color of each area your lights follow, and discards the picture. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Audio. For music sync, it listens to the sound your Mac plays and measures its loudness, bass, mids, highs and beats. The audio itself is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Settings. Your preferences, light layouts and colors are stored on your Mac, along with the pairing key your Hue Bridge issues.

What goes over the network

  • Your Hue Bridge. Blask Sync talks to your Hue Bridge directly on your local network: it reads your lights and entertainment areas and sends them colors. This never leaves your network.
  • Bridge discovery. To find your bridge, it looks on your local network and may ask discovery.meethue.com, the Philips Hue discovery service run by Signify, which bridges are on your network. That request goes to Signify like any web request, with your IP address; Blask Sync sends nothing else. Signify's own privacy notice covers that service.
  • Updates. The version downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub for new versions once a day (you can turn this off in Settings). The Mac App Store version is updated by the App Store.

Permissions

macOS asks for Screen Recording (for screen sync), System Audio Recording (for music sync) and Local Network access (to reach your bridge). Each is used only for the purpose above, and you can withdraw it at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Children

Blask Sync collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
