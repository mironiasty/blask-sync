**Description** [4000]:

Blask Sync syncs your Philips Hue lights with your Mac: with the picture on your screen, like an ambilight, or
with the music your Mac plays. It lives in the menu bar and stays out of the way.

SCREEN SYNC
The lights behind and around your monitor follow the edges of the picture. Place each light on a canvas of your
desk, drag it where it really is, and it follows that part of the screen. Gradient lightstrips get a separate
color for every segment. Choose how fast the lights react, from a calm glow to fast enough for games.

MUSIC SYNC
Your lights move with whatever your Mac plays, from any app, without extra drivers. Colors flow along the
strip and swell with the beat, in palettes from Vivid to Ocean.

MANUAL
Set each light's color on a color wheel, or pick a scene, right from the menu bar.

THOUGHTFUL TOUCHES
• Turns the lights off when your Mac sleeps or shuts down, and back on when it wakes
• Opens at login if you like
• Pauses for the Apple TV app, so protected movies keep playing
• In English, Polish, German, Spanish and French

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no analytics. Your screen and audio are analyzed on your Mac and never leave it; only colors go to
your Hue Bridge, over your own network.

REQUIREMENTS
• A Philips Hue Bridge (v2 or Pro) on the same network as your Mac
• Color Hue lights in an entertainment area, set up in the Philips Hue app
• macOS 14.2 or later

Data collection: **No, we do not collect data from this app.** (→ "Data Not Collected"). Matches

## Age rating

Every question "None" / "No" (no user-generated content, no web access, no purchases) → **4+**.

## Export compliance

The app uses only standard encryption built into macOS (HTTPS and DTLS to the Hue Bridge), so it's exempt;
`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is `false` in Info.plist and App Store Connect won't ask.

## App Review information

**Sign-in required**: No.

**Notes**:

> Blask Sync controls Philips Hue lights through a Philips Hue Bridge on the local network, using the public Hue
> API (CLIP v2 and the Hue Entertainment streaming API, documented at developers.meethue.com). It needs that
> hardware to do anything useful, so we attached a video, recorded on a Mac, that shows the whole flow: finding
> and pairing the bridge (pressing its link button), choosing an entertainment area, screen sync with the lights
> following the picture, music sync, manual control, and the lights turning off when sync stops.
>
> Without a bridge, you can still open the menu bar icon (a monitor with a sparkle) → Pair Bridge… → Settings,
> where the app searches the network and explains what it needs.
>
> Permissions: Screen Recording (screen sync), System Audio Recording (music sync), Local Network (finding and
> controlling the bridge). Nothing is collected or sent anywhere except colors to the bridge.
>
> Blask Sync is an independent app, not affiliated with Signify, and uses the name Philips Hue only to say which
> lights it works with.

**Attachment**: a demo video (filmed with a phone so both the Mac screen and the lights are visible — Apple asks
for a recording of the real hardware, not only a screen recording), 1–3 minutes, showing everything in the notes.
